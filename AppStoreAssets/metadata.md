## Description (max 4000 chars)

```
Nihongo News turns today's real world news into personalized Japanese language lessons — tailored exactly to your JLPT level.

HOW IT WORKS
Every article is generated fresh from current events, then rewritten in natural Japanese at your exact learning level. New vocabulary is highlighted inline so you can tap any word to see its meaning, reading, and examples. After reading, a short quiz tests what you learned.

FEATURES
• Real news — articles sourced from actual current events, not made-up practice sentences
• Your JLPT level — articles adapt vocabulary and grammar complexity to N5, N4, N3, N2, or N1
• Three writing systems — read in Romaji, Hiragana/Katakana, or full Kanji with furigana
• Tap-to-translate — tap any highlighted word to see its meaning and romaji reading
• Vocabulary tracking — the app remembers which words you know, which you're learning
• Spaced repetition quizzes — review new words before you forget them
• Topic variety — technology, sports, culture, food, science, politics, and more
• Multiple regions — news from Japan, Germany, the US, and beyond

PERFECT FOR
Students studying for JLPT N5 through N1, self-learners who find textbook examples boring, and anyone who wants to read real Japanese content without being overwhelmed.

No subscription required. Generate articles whenever you want to study.
```

## What's New in 1.3

```
New ways to really learn the words you read:

• Build Sentences — write your own sentences with an article's new words and get instant corrections, with a one-line explanation for each fix.
• Daily Review — type 10 words from memory. Words you miss come back at the end. No macrons needed.
• Tap any word — tap any word in an article to see its meaning in that sentence, and add it to your words.
• Harder vocabulary quiz — English → Japanese questions, trickier answer choices, and your weakest words first.
• Article writing screen — see what's happening while your article is written. You can leave the app and find it waiting.
• Grammar points are now shown in romaji when you read in romaji.
• Fixes: correct JLPT levels for common verbs like sagaru and agaru, and a clearer free-article count.
```
