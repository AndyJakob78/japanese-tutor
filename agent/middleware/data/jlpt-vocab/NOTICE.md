# JLPT vocabulary lists

`n5_vocab_cleaned.csv` … `n1_vocab_cleaned.csv` are copied unchanged from
[Bluskyo/JLPT_Vocabulary](https://github.com/Bluskyo/JLPT_Vocabulary)
(commit `4358f932937ad0232194a36e9f4f875094910c6b`, `data/vocab/parsedData/`),
downloaded 2026-11-03.

- Word data: JLPT vocabulary lists by **Jonathan Waller**, https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/ —
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).
- Conversion to CSV: © 2026 Bluskyo, MIT License (see `LICENSE-Bluskyo-MIT`).

JLPT has not published official word lists since 2010; these are the standard
community reference. Used by [../jlpt-levels.js](../jlpt-levels.js) as the fallback
level lookup behind the hand-curated list.
