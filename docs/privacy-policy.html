Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Japanese AI News Learner ("the app") is a personal Japanese language learning app. This privacy policy describes what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

The app stores the following data on the server to provide its service:

  • A device identifier — a random ID generated on first launch, used to associate your articles and vocabulary with your account. No name, email, or personal identity is collected.
  • Your learning settings — JLPT level, preferred topics, regions, writing system preference.
  • Generated articles — the articles generated for your account, including vocabulary words and quiz results.
  • Places for your articles — if you allow location access, the app looks up your approximate position once on your device and turns it into a city, region and country name, which it suggests as places for your articles. Only the place names you choose are stored; your coordinates are never sent to our server. If you do not allow location access, places are suggested from your time zone.
  • Setup answers — once, at the end of setup: your phone language, time zone, the places and topics you chose, which setup steps you skipped, and (if you answer) where you heard about the app. We use this to understand how learners find and set up the app.
  • Apple Ads attribution — if you installed the app after tapping an Apple Ads ad, Apple's AdServices framework gives the app a token. Our server exchanges it with Apple for the campaign, ad group and keyword IDs and the country of the ad. This tells us which ad worked; it contains no personal information and is not used to track you.
  • Invite codes — your device's invite code, and which code (if any) you entered, so both devices can receive their extra free article.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not collect your precise location or GPS coordinates.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the Anthropic Claude API to generate article content. Article generation requests (your topic preferences, JLPT level, and recent article history) are sent to Anthropic's servers. When you use Build Sentences or tap a word to look it up, the sentence you wrote (or the tapped word and its sentence from the article) is sent to Anthropic to check it or explain it. These texts are not stored on our server, except that a word you add to your vocabulary is saved with its sentence from the article. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

The backend is hosted on Google Cloud Run and uses Google Firestore for data storage. Your data is stored in Google's infrastructure. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Subscriptions and payments are managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymised device identifier to manage your subscription entitlement. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. All payment processing is handled by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Ads attribution uses Apple's AdServices framework, and place names are looked up with Apple's location services on your device; both are subject to Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Data Retention

Your data (articles, vocabulary, settings) is stored indefinitely so the app can track your learning progress. You can delete individual articles within the app. To request deletion of all your data, contact us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Significant changes will be noted in the app's App Store release notes.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request data deletion, open an issue at github.com/AndyJakob78/japanese-tutor.
